# Model cards

Generated by `python -X utf8 -m src.make_model_cards` from completed run records.

## Shared facts

All models: PTB-XL v1.0.3, official patient folds (1-8 train, 9 validation, 10 test), five overlapping superclasses (NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP), training-only normalization, AdamW lr 1e-3, batch 16, max 20 epochs, patience 4, cosine schedule, unweighted BCE, no augmentation, no pretrained weights, checkpoint selected on validation, threshold 0.5, one test inference. Fold 10 was observed before the models were specified, so test results are exploratory. Intended use: research on ECG-image modelling only, not clinical use. Known limitations: clean synthetic renderings (no scans/photos), one population (Germany), label noise, and adaptive page gain on v1 pages leaks amplitude (and therefore hypertrophy) information.

## Models

- **PCA + LDA / PCA + linear SVM.** Classical baselines on PCA of the downsampled page; one fit each (no seeds). Hyperparameters chosen on validation by a frozen grid.
- **CNN-only (cnn_pool).** Shared 1,220-parameter lead-image CNN, mean over 70 feature columns, linear readout. 1,705 parameters. Sees only local image regions; cannot relate the calibration pulse to the waveform.
- **Wide CNN-only (cnn_pool_wide).** Same CNN, per-column MLP 96-96-64 then column mean. 17,065 parameters: capacity of the sequence heads without column mixing (protocol amendment 3 Nov 2026).
- **CNN-LSTM (cnn_lstm).** Same CNN, one unidirectional LSTM layer (hidden width 32) over the 70 columns, mean of outputs. 18,025 parameters.
- **CNN + temporal convolution (temporal_conv).** Same CNN, two causal dilated residual Conv1d blocks (receptive field 13 columns). 17,161 parameters.
- **CNN + small ViT (hybrid_vit).** Same CNN, small transformer encoder over the columns with positional embeddings. 23,881 parameters. Sees the whole row at once.
- **Signal ResNet1D (resnet1d_wang).** Reference model on the raw 100 Hz waveforms instead of images. About 510k parameters. Shows what the image representation costs.

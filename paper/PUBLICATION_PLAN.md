# ECG image benchmark publication plan

Planning window: **1–30 November 2026**. Target: **a submission-ready manuscript package for supervisor review on 1 December 2026**. Submission itself depends on completed evidence, author agreement and venue requirements. No publication acceptance or deadline is implied.

## Research question and current claim

On a fixed patient-separated PTB-XL image benchmark, how do compact statistical, spatial-CNN, recurrent, temporal-convolution and hybrid-attention classifiers compare in ranking performance, class sensitivity, stability and computation? A useful contribution would be an auditable, controlled comparison with clear representation and uncertainty limits. A higher score from one seed or a routine combination of known layers is not sufficient novelty by itself.

Five formal runs are verified: three four-class Mendeley experiments and two five-label PTB-XL experiments. The spatiotemporal PTB-XL model is provisionally stronger overall than CNN–LSTM, while HYP sensitivity declines. A compact hybrid ViT is actively training under a frozen protocol and has no completed outcome yet. See [experiment notes](EXPERIMENT_NOTES.md) for exact results. The historical manuscript's numerical claims are excluded unless their original provenance is separately recovered and verified.

Make PTB-XL the main comparison because its explicit patient folds and overlapping diagnostic labels support a consistent task. Present Mendeley as a separate preliminary dataset-audit/representation case study or supplement; do not create one cross-dataset model leaderboard. Its supplied classes, duplicate burden, uncertain printed-ID semantics and unadjudicated clinical labels limit stronger claims.

## Protocol decisions to freeze before new fits

- **Matched PTB-XL baselines:** use the same cached images, patients, labels and exclusions. A CNN-only baseline can retain the shared spatial encoder with a fixed pooled five-logit readout. A multilabel LDA baseline needs five prespecified binary discriminants and train-only PCA/feature fitting, not the existing four-class classifier. Record any deterministic image reduction as a representation difference; quantify its effect before calling the comparison architecturally controlled.
- **Selection and outcomes:** retain validation macro AUROC as primary, macro AP then negative BCE as deterministic ties, and earliest epoch for exact ties. Report macro/micro/weighted F1, per-label precision/recall/specificity, exact match and label accuracy separately. LDA has a direct fit, not a fictional epoch or optimizer budget.
- **Seeds:** at least three prespecified seeds characterize initial training variability. Do not choose the “best seed.” Present seed-level observations and uncertainty at the correct level. Patient-bootstrap intervals conditional on one fitted model do not measure seed variability. A deterministic LDA fit does not gain independent evidence merely by repeating the identical estimator; distinguish randomized PCA variability if studied.
- **Thresholds and imbalance:** retain the established 0.5 results as the primary reference. If training weights, sampling, calibration or label thresholds are investigated, freeze candidate values and an inner development split/CV design before fitting. Fit choices on training/inner-development only, then assess on reserved development data. A threshold optimized and reported on the same validation cases is optimistic and must be labelled accordingly. Test outcomes never choose thresholds or weights.
- **Test exposure:** the official fold-10 test has already influenced the research context, so further comparisons remain exploratory even with one final inference per frozen model. Store probabilities once and reuse them for statistics. A new confirmatory claim requires independent untouched data or a genuinely prespecified nested evaluation. Keep a chronological protocol/run record and make this limitation explicit.
- **Preservation:** each run gets a new ID, frozen configuration/source hashes, a current prior-artifact/log baseline, checkpoint, history, predictions and failure/completion state. Existing run directories and historical preservation locks remain unchanged. A new ID alone is insufficient when an old runner is bound to an earlier log snapshot.

## External validation and scope decision

An external cohort is useful only with independent patients, compatible diagnostic endpoints, documented labels and permission to use it. Freeze the evaluation before seeing its predictions. Real scans/photos would test the intended paper-image application; another rendering of PTB-XL can test rendering robustness but cannot establish independent-population or real-scan generalization. The four Mendeley categories do not map automatically to PTB-XL's five superclasses; do not relabel them to manufacture an external accuracy comparison.

If no suitable independent cohort is secured by 21 November, finish a bounded synthetic-rendered-image benchmark with explicit transfer limitations. Do not delay all writing for unconfirmed hospital access, and do not imply that external validation occurred. A degradation study on held-out patients is a controlled stress test, not clinical deployment validation.

## Claim and submission checks

The strongest plausible near-term claim is reproducible comparison of compact models on a defined rendered PTB-XL task, with stable effects and clear per-label failures if the new experiments support it. Without matched baselines and repeated seeds, describe the evidence as exploratory. Without an external cohort, avoid claims about scan/photo robustness or clinical performance. Hybrid attention and temporal convolution are established methods; demonstrate a useful evaluation or representation contribution rather than naming the combination as novel.

Use the new [bibliography](references.bib), which records checked primary sources and corrected historical metadata. Recheck final citation requirements and source availability at submission.
